## Claim, in my words

Prefix conditioning during fine-tuning helps the model recognize latent properties of the training data, which, during test-time, can be toggled off by not adding the prefix.

- > a single general inoculation (“You are a malicious, evil assistant”) almost completely mitigates the extent of emergent mis-alignment from three separate narrow datasets

Main exciting usage is for mitigating emergent misalignment.

## This would be wrong if

- This feels like it would only work at later stages of post-training, where the model has the capability to fully understand what the inoculation is about?


## What I didn't understand

- What if one puts a completely irrelevant inoculation as training data? What happens?
- > in the GPT-4.1 Spanish + capitalization setting, spanish-inoc impairs the learning of capitalization. This does not occur in Qwen. 
	- Is this a model capacity “confound” where larger models are harder to steer in this way? What could be the cause?
- Seems like all the experiments in the paper, at least, were mostly toy?
