## Summary

Intuition: Given domain $A$ and $B$ where there is unintentially some associated feature/condition $C_A$ and $C_B$ with each respective domain’s prepared training data $X_A$ and $X_B$, where $C_A(x) = 1$ for $x \in X_A$ (and similarly for $B$). 
While the original training objective is for the model to learn $f_A(x)$ and $f_B(x)$ for any $x$, the model instead learns to use $C_A$ and $C_B$ as cues for when to invoke those behaviors, e.g. $C_A(x)=1 \Rightarrow f_A(x)$ and $C_B(x)=1 \Rightarrow f_B(x)$. Then, when an instance $x \in B$ also satisfies $C_A(x)=1$, the model spuriously invokes behavior associated with $f_A$.
