## Summary

Hypothesizes that in the instruction-tuning + post-training on narrow malicious data regime, models can learn to associate the chat template prefix with the undesirable behavior and universally invoke the undesirable behavior afterwards, thus causing emergent misalignment. Paper refers to this as the “piggyback hypothesis.”



## Questions/Comments
- Can this be mitigated by simply mixing in different forms of training data during this specific post-training step?
- Feels like (in a very loose sense) a form of “reward gaming.” (model trivially learns to associate specific behavior to prefix)
