## Summary

Proposes training method for agentic safety against malicious user requests, as chat safety does not reliably transfer to executing harmful tasks through tools. 

SFT on Ministral-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Functionary-Small-v3.2. Data: Handwritten seed behavior chains → synthetic expansion via LLMs → manual filtering. Final 18,749 examples: 4,956 harmful, 9,783 benign, 4,010 ToolACE/Glaive examples. AgentHarm harmful-task scores goes down.
